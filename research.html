Autonomous Intelligence Systems
Pushing the boundary of edge AI inference, multi-agent coordination, and situation-aware decision engines that operate with zero cloud latency in safety-critical environments. Our focus spans neuromorphic hardware co-design, federated learning under bandwidth constraints, and adversarial robustness in real-world deployments.

Sovereign Infrastructure & Cryptography
Developing post-quantum cryptographic protocols, zero-knowledge data vaults, and decentralized consensus mechanisms for national-scale digital sovereignty. Our cryptography lab collaborates with government agencies to replace foreign-controlled infrastructure with open, verifiable alternatives.
Post-Quantum ZK Proofs Blockchain PKI

03
Robotic Systems & Bio-Inspired Motion
Researching legged locomotion over unstructured terrain, swarm coordination algorithms, and human-robot collaboration protocols for real-world deployments. Drawing from biomechanics and evolutionary algorithms to create robots that navigate environments humans cannot.
Locomotion Swarm AI SLAM HRI

Planetary IoT & Earth Intelligence
Building the sensor networks, compression algorithms, and AI inference pipelines that transform raw environmental data into actionable intelligence at planetary scale. Research spans ultra-low-power sensor design, LPWAN protocol optimization, and AI-driven anomaly detection from satellite and ground sensor fusion.
