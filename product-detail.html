System Overview

The AN Sentinel is Astranova's flagship AI-powered surveillance and situational awareness platform, engineered for national security, border management, and critical infrastructure protection. Unlike conventional CCTV or imported surveillance hardware, AN Sentinel runs entirely on sovereign infrastructure — no video data leaves the deployment country.

At its core, the platform fuses data from optical cameras, thermal imagers, acoustic sensors, and radar modules into a single real-time intelligence picture. A custom-trained edge AI model — running on our AN Cortex compute unit — detects, classifies, and tracks threats without any cloud connection, achieving sub-200ms alert latency even in contested or denied environments.

AN Sentinel scales from a single hardened installation to a networked perimeter spanning hundreds of kilometers, all managed from a single web-based command dashboard with role-based access, full audit trails, and encrypted communications throughout.

Core Capabilities
  • Real-time AI object detection and classification across 50+ threat categories including humans, vehicles, watercraft, and low-altitude aircraft
  • Multi-sensor data fusion: optical, thermal, acoustic, radar, and millimeter-wave inputs unified in one intelligence layer
  • Sovereign edge processing — all computation on-premise, zero data exfiltration
  • Automated threat escalation with configurable response protocols and multi-channel alerting
  • Encrypted mesh communications between nodes using AN Shield cryptographic stack
  • Hardened hardware rated for −40°C to +70°C, IP67, MIL-STD-810H
  • Offline operation: full capability maintained with zero internet connectivity
  • Web-based command center with role-based access, audit log, and live replay

Deployment Scenarios

Use Cases

Border Security
Continuous 24/7 monitoring of land borders with automated detection of unauthorized crossings, tunnel activity, and vehicle incursions — reducing manpower requirements by up to 60%.
Critical Infrastructure
Protecting power plants, dams, airports, government buildings, and data centers against physical intrusion, drone overflights, and perimeter breaches.
Urban Surveillance
City-scale monitoring networks with real-time crowd analytics, incident detection, and integration with emergency dispatch systems — maintaining privacy by design through on-device processing.

Deployed in national parks and conservation zones to detect poaching activity, illegal logging, and wildfire ignition — with specialized models trained on local fauna.
