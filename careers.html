What We Offer

Benefits & Perks

Sovereign-Grade Health Cover
Full medical, dental, and vision insurance for you and your dependants. Annual wellness credit of NPR 50,000 for gym, therapy, or preventive care.
Learning & Development
NPR 1.5L annual education budget. We sponsor certifications, conference talks, and advanced degrees. Internal R&D fridays — 20% time for your own experiments.
Flexible Working Hours
Core hours 10AM–3PM, otherwise schedule your own day. Remote-friendly roles available. Result-oriented culture — we measure output, not hours.
Equity & Bonus
Performance-linked bonuses up to 25% of base salary. Employee stock option pool (ESOP) for senior roles. Annual salary review with transparent band progression.
World-Class Hardware
Top-of-the-line development machines, lab access to drones, robots, and RF equipment. No waiting for procurement — if you need it to build, you get it.
Mission Trips & Field Work
Deployments to national parks, border regions, rural farms, and smart city projects. See your systems running in the wild — not just in a demo environment.
Simple & Transparent

Our Hiring Process

01
Apply
Send us your portfolio, GitHub, or relevant work samples. No generic cover letters needed — show us what you've built.
02
Intro Call
A 30-min call with our hiring lead. We talk about your work, your goals, and what the role actually involves day-to-day.
03
Technical
A focused technical exercise relevant to the role — no whiteboard puzzle nonsense. Usually 3–4 hours of real, meaningful work.
04
Team Meet
Meet the people you'd be working with. Ask hard questions. We want the fit to be mutual.
05
Offer
Transparent offer with full compensation breakdown. No lowball anchoring — we lead with our best number.
